Our Story

Good Food Brings People Together

Cafe S21 is an open-air garden cafe in Koregaon Bhima, built for slow evenings and loud laughter. Wooden benches, green palms, warm lanterns and sparkling chandeliers make it a place you'll want to stay a little longer.

Bring your family, your friends or your special someone. We'll bring hot, freshly made food and chilled drinks at prices that keep everyone smiling.
